Cooking is a wonderful way to explore different flavours. Here are some popular dishes and their basic ingredients.

1. Chicken Biryani

Chicken biryani is a delicious rice dish prepared with chicken, basmati rice, spices and herbs.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken
  • Basmati Rice
  • Onion and Tomato
  • Ginger and Garlic
  • Spices and Herbs

2. Masala Dosa

Masala dosa is prepared using fermented rice and lentil batter. It is served with potato masala, sambar and chutney.

Ingredients:

  • Rice and Urad Dal Batter
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Spices

3. Pizza

Pizza is prepared using dough, tomato sauce, cheese and various toppings. It is baked until the crust is cooked and the cheese melts.

Ingredients:

  • Pizza Base
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Vegetables
  • Seasoning
